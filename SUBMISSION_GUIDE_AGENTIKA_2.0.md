## Section 2: About Your Project
* **Project Name:** LawFlow
* **One-Line Pitch:** Autonomous Judicial Intake, Scheduling & Human-in-the-Loop Case Management System designed to eliminate court delays and failure-to-appear rates while enforcing strict human civil rights checkpoints.
* **The Problem & Who It Is For (2–3 Lines):**  
  Courts worldwide face crippling administrative backlogs where manual citation intake takes 5+ days and failure-to-appear rates exceed 39%, leading to unfair default arrest warrants. LawFlow is built for municipal court clerks, magistrates, and citation respondents to streamline legal intake, notice dispatch, and calendar assignment in under 12 seconds.
* **Where the Human Comes In (Theme: "Human + AI: Better Together"):**  
  Judicial authority cannot be delegated to an unsupervised model. LawFlow enforces two non-negotiable **Human-in-the-Loop Gates**:
  1. **Gate 1 (Judicial Match & Hearing Sign-Off):** The AI ranks magistrates and computes an AI Reasoning Card, but the Court Clerk must review and confirm or override the assignment before any summons is dispatched.
  2. **Gate 2 (Police Bench Warrant Authorization):** If a respondent fails to respond after 72 hours, the AI compiles a digital Bench Warrant packet, but only a human Clerk can authorize its formal referral to law enforcement.
* **What You Built With:**  
  * **Framework & Code:** Custom Python 3.11 + FastAPI + SQLite state engine + HTML5/CSS3 glassmorphic SPA with 60fps neural canvas visualizer.
  * **AI Models:** Groq Cloud (`openai/gpt-oss-120b` / `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`) with automatic fallback to Google Gemini 1.5 Flash and a deterministic offline legal reasoning matrix.
  * **APIs & Protocols:** Model Context Protocol (MCP) v1.0 (6 codified legal tools), Web Speech API (Text-to-Speech), Virtual Telephony SMS & Real-Time Email dispatch engine.
